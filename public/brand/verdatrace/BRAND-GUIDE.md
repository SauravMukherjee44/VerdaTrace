# VerdaTrace brand guide

## Brand foundation

**Name:** VerdaTrace  
**Primary tagline:** Every ecological obligation, traceable.  
**Supporting message:** From ecological promise to verifiable action.

VerdaTrace is an AI-native environmental obligation intelligence platform. It
connects environmental approvals, amendments, maps, evidence, and expert review
into a traceable path from obligation to action.

## Symbol

The traceleaf mark combines:

- A `V` for VerdaTrace.
- A leaf-like outer path for ecological context.
- Connected evidence nodes for source traceability.
- Multiple paths converging at one reviewed record.

The symbol should remain unchanged. Do not remove nodes, redraw the paths, add a
checkmark, place the mark inside a generic shield, or combine it with another
logo.

## Color system

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary forest | Deep forest green | `#0F2A22` |
| Dark surface | Charcoal | `#101714` |
| Light surface | Warm ivory | `#F3F1E8` |
| Secondary detail | Muted sage | `#90A99C` |
| Priority signal | Signal amber | `#C88B3A` |
| Evidence signal | Evidence blue | `#607D8B` |

Amber should indicate a review priority, revision, or unresolved evidence path.
Blue should indicate mapped or supporting evidence. Neither color should imply a
legal compliance determination.

## Typography

- Preserve the supplied wordmark artwork instead of retyping it.
- Product and marketing UI: `Geist Sans`, with `Inter` as a compatible fallback.
- Technical labels, clause IDs, dates, and coordinates: `Geist Mono`.
- Use sentence case. Avoid all-caps paragraphs and decorative serif type.

## Logo usage

- Use `logo-primary-transparent.png` on warm ivory or other light surfaces.
- Use `logo-reverse-dark.png` on dark forest surfaces and presentation covers.
- Use `mark-transparent.png` for favicons, avatars, app icons, and compact UI.
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least one large endpoint-node
  diameter from the symbol.
- Do not place the logo over visually busy maps without a solid or glass-backed
  holding area.

## Voice

VerdaTrace should sound precise, grounded, and accountable.

Prefer:

- Source-grounded
- Human-reviewed
- Evidence coverage
- Current obligation
- Missing evidence
- Prioritized verification

Avoid:

- Guaranteed compliance
- AI-certified
- Fully verified
- Restoration proven
- Regulator approved

## Approved launch copy

**Headline:** Every ecological obligation, traceable.

**One-line description:** VerdaTrace turns environmental approvals, amendments,
maps, and field evidence into source-cited obligations and prioritized action.

**Short description:** AI-native environmental obligation intelligence with
clause-level citations, amendment resolution, spatial evidence gaps, and human
approval.
