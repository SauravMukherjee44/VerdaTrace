# Two-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

“Ecological promises are not usually broken in one dramatic moment. They become
hard to verify across approvals, amendments, maps, and field files. VerdaTrace
turns those scattered records into one reviewable evidence system.”

## 0:20–0:45 — The public case

Open the public Ballari road case. Point to the revision card.

“The May 2025 approval required compensatory afforestation over 9.54 hectares.
In September, that same parcel was reassigned to natural growth and wildlife
habitat. VerdaTrace preserves the original clause as superseded and makes
only the amended clause active. All other conditions remain unchanged.”

## 0:45–1:15 — Cited ledger

Open the obligation ledger and filter to “Missing evidence.”

“Every finding has the responsible party, quantity, timing, geography, and a
page-level citation. Absent proof is called missing evidence—not
non-compliance. A consultant can approve or reject each AI-assisted finding.”

## 1:15–1:35 — Spatial honesty

Return to Overview and show the map.

“The proposal page records KML files, but the amended parcel geometry is not
reliably available in this review set. The map therefore shows only the
documented approximate location and makes the geometry gap explicit.”

## 1:35–1:52 — Inspection plan

Open Inspection Plan.

“The planner ranks unresolved duties by ecological importance and verification
effort, then creates a field-ready checklist: what to inspect, where, what
evidence to capture, and a safety note.”

## 1:52–2:00 — Live AI and close

Open Analyze PDF.

“Gemini reads a new PDF natively and returns validated, cited obligations for
human review. VerdaTrace moves environmental review from document hunting
to targeted action.”
